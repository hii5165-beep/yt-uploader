yt-uploader Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

yt-uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool accesses the owner's YouTube channel in order to: upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists, upload caption files, and read the status of uploaded videos and basic channel information (channel name and ID).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform the uploads the owner requests. Access tokens and an upload log are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's token files on the owner's computer also removes stored data.
